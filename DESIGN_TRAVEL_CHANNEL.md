# DEAD AIR / the travel-channel career

Start a fictional YouTube travel channel anywhere during the journey, including by pulling over mid-drive. The family is escaping uninsurable Cape Coral for Seattle in 2028. A creator career, its equipment, draft footage, uploads, subscribers, orders, and accounts survive leaving the studio and moving between stops within that journey.

## Play loop

Name the channel for free. Use the existing phone or order equipment from **V&H — Victor & Horoshilov**. Orders debit road cash immediately and arrive after one trail day. Spend a day and mobile-data money making a video; its idea is randomly assigned from the authored pool. Publish it, inspect audience and earnings, and decide whether to sink more days and money into the channel. A draft can travel with you, but location-specific footage can only be captured while arrived at the matching stop. Browsing, publishing, and returning to the road do not spend days. Discarding footage never refunds its cost.

## Simulation promises

- Twenty actual Luna agents at xhigh effort author ten ideas each, against a shared growing pool with collision rejection. Exactly 200 unique ideas have server-only potential scores and explicit location restrictions. Most ideas are deliberately dull.
- The player cannot choose an idea or see its potential score. Weak and middling ideas have hard audience-demand limits. Only excellent ideas can break out, and even those usually disappoint.
- Subscribers and non-followers are separately randomized on every original upload. Subscriber growth/churn and discovery response both change a bounded, hidden recommendation multiplier. Neither that multiplier nor idea scores are sent to the browser.
- Equipment only changes a maximum audience capacity. Identical random draws below the cheaper kit's ceiling produce identical views. Lights, SD cards, batteries, displays, lenses, ND filters, audio, and supports have satirical price/spec tradeoffs. Only the strongest equipped item in each supporting category contributes; lenses require an interchangeable camera and the correct mount; HDMI monitors require a compatible camera.
- Original uploads have an independent 15% demonetization roll, with an explicit incident such as family profanity or unnoticed public exposure. An affected video can be re-edited once for one trail day. Its replacement gets exactly floor(original views / 2), without replaying subscriber acquisition, and no second random moderation roll. Original and replacement are distinct audience history; only the repaired upload can earn ad revenue.
- Monetization uses explicitly fictional in-game thresholds. Ad earnings accumulate and can be transferred to road cash once a payout minimum is reached. Gear, production costs, and unclaimed earnings appear in an honest profit/loss ledger.
- Calibrate approximately 8% net-positive careers against a declared, reproducible frugal filming policy. Also measure gear-spending and longer careers; do not force an invisible predetermined winner flag or guarantee 8% for every strategy.

## Verification

Seeded rule tests: weak ideas never become viral; gear changes only ceilings; mounts and monitor compatibility; costs and delivery; exact-once publishing/withdrawal/re-edit; 15% moderation frequency; half-view reupload; both audience sources and hidden score movement; geography while arrived versus departed; complete unique pool and actual author provenance. HTTP tests: start while traveling, preserve state across returns, revision/replay rules, days without miles, no hidden scores in payloads, isolation. Browser checks: complete loop and usable narrow-screen layouts. All state follows the existing memory-only journey lifetime.
